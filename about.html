Where it Begins

Welcome to a realm where magic whispers, hidden doors appear, and the bravest hero of all is a cat. Mouse's Enchanted Adventures is an immersive fantasy world brought to life by author Katherine McNeil.

The Enchanted World

This whimsical tale follows Mouse, a vampire tabby cat, as he moves through danger, friendship, mystery, and magic with Katherina, Devlin, Bat, Ivera, and Christan by his side.

Characters

Meet the enchanted cast from Mouse's magical world.

Mouse the Vampire Cat

Mouse is brave, curious, and full of personality. Behind his green eyes and red bowtie is a hero wrapped in tabby stripes.

Bat the Loyal Familiar

Bat is loyal, funny, and always nearby when Mouse needs him most. He brings mischief, heart, and magical support.

Devlin the Young Warlock

Devlin is clever, curious, and growing into his powers. He may be the key to unlocking ancient magic.

Katherina the Enchanted Witch

Katherina is elegant, wise, and powerful. Her calm nature hides a past filled with trials and secrets.

Ivera

Ivera is Devlin's older sister. Sarcastic, playful, and confident, she brings plenty of personality and wit to every adventure.

Christan

Christan is Devlin's older brother, an exceptionally intelligent wizard with a quick wit and a talent for understanding complicated magic.

The Creator

The imagination behind Mouse's magical universe.

Katherine McNeil

Katherine McNeil is a writer, creator, and imaginative force behind the Mouse universe. With a love of storytelling and fantasy, she brings Mouse and his world to life one magical page at a time.

Why Mouse Matters

Through Mouse, readers are invited to embrace wonder, follow curiosity, and never underestimate a cat in a cape.
